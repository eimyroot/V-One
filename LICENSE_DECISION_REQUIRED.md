# License decision required before public sale

No software license is selected by this engineering bundle.

Before external distribution, choose and have counsel review one of these models:

- proprietary commercial license,
- source-available license with commercial terms,
- dual licensing,
- open-source core plus commercial enterprise modules.

Do not publish the repository or distribute binaries to customers without an explicit license, EULA, privacy notice and data-processing terms appropriate to the delivery model.
